Golf Labs Backed by Y Combinator

Golf Labs collects video and tactile data from real work across Europe, for robot training.

We believe touch belongs in pretraining, not just fine-tuning. Video shows what hands do; touch shows how hard, and when. That can't come from a lab. It has to be tactile in the wild.

And we believe robots should learn from the best: human experts, doing real work, in the places where it's done.
